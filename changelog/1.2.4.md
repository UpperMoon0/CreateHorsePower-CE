# Create Horse Power - CE 1.2.4

## Verification

- Added two worker-first recovery regression scenarios for issue #4, each mirrored across Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1.
- The live-owner regression proves recovery remains queued while the recorded crank chunk is unavailable, never force-loads that chunk, and preserves attachment plus AI-suppression ownership and `NoAI=true` when the matching crank becomes available later.
- The complementary orphan regression proves the same deferred worker-first ordering recovers immediately once the delayed chunk becomes inspectable with no matching owner: stale attachment/activity ownership is cleared and the worker's original `NoAI=false` state is restored before the 1200-tick timeout.
- Both worker-first regressions now rely on the real loader `EntityJoinLevelEvent` hook to enqueue the reloaded worker instead of manually scheduling recovery from the test.
- Together these regressions close the remaining coverage gap around the recovery behavior introduced in 1.2.1; no worker-recovery gameplay semantics are changed in this release.
